# Playosmo evidence

Use this folder for approved artifacts that support the Playosmo case study in the repository root.

## Suggested contents

- `screenshots/` — approved storefront or product screenshots
- `design/` — selected mockups, flows, or visual explorations
- `diagrams/` — architecture, process, or user-flow diagrams
- `notes/` — concise explanations of decisions and trade-offs

For each artifact, include what it is, what it demonstrates, your contribution, and whether it is a final result or an iteration. Add project-specific Shopify or implementation details only when they are accurate and safe to share.

## Before publishing

- Confirm you have permission to publish the artifact.
- Remove secrets, credentials, personal information, and customer data.
- Redact internal or proprietary details.
- Credit third-party assets and collaborators as appropriate.
- Do not present mockups or sample data as shipped product or measured results.
